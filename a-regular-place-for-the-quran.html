A regular place for the Qur’an

A little preparation can help us return to reading with more attention and consistency.

The journal2 Min readEditorial preview
← All articlesThe reflectionA practical stepFurther reflection

كِتَابٌ أَنزَلْنَاهُ إِلَيْكَ مُبَارَكٌ

QUR'AN · Sad 38:29

There are days when we read with attention, and days when our thoughts are elsewhere. Building a relationship with the Qur’an means continuing to return, while learning how to give it the attention it deserves.

Sad 38:29 describes the Book as blessed and points to reflection on its verses. This gives our reading a purpose beyond completing a page or reaching a number.

A useful beginning is to choose a time you can realistically protect. You might read after a prayer, before checking your phone in the morning or during a quiet part of the evening. The time itself matters less than the care you bring to it.

Read with Attention

Put distractions aside when you can. Read at a pace that allows you to notice the words. If you are learning Arabic, continue to seek help with recitation and meaning from qualified teachers. A trusted translation and reliable Tafseer can support your understanding.

Keep Learning

When a question arises, write it down rather than rushing to your own conclusion. Bring it to a teacher or a reliable explanation. Let each reading lead naturally into the next opportunity to learn.
