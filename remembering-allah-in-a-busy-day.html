Remembering Allah in a busy day

How to make room for remembrance without waiting for life to slow down.

The journal2 Min readEditorial preview
← All articlesThe reflectionA practical stepFurther reflection

أَلَا بِذِكْرِ اللَّهِ تَطْمَئِنُّ الْقُلُوبُ

QUR'AN · Ar-Ra'd 13:28

So much of the day asks for our attention. Messages arrive, tasks expand and moments of silence are quickly filled. It can become difficult to notice how much noise we are carrying.

Ar-Ra’d 13:28 connects the tranquillity of believing hearts with the remembrance of Allah. It draws our attention back to a relationship that deserves space within the day.

Making that space need not begin with a dramatic change. It might mean putting the phone aside after prayer, paying attention to the words of our remembrance or returning to a passage of the Qur’an without rushing to the next task.

A Pause for Today

Choose one familiar moment in your routine. Let it become a cue to pause and remember Allah. Keep your intention simple and your attention present. If you become distracted, return gently to what you are doing.

Carry the Reminder with You

Read the verse in its context and continue learning its meaning. A short reflection can open a door to further study, while regular engagement with the Qur’an helps give that learning a place in everyday life.
