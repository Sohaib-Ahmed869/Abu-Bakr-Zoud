Holding on to hope during hardship

A reflection on the reassurance in Surah Ash-Sharh, and taking the next small step.

The journal2 Min readEditorial preview
← All articlesThe reflectionA practical stepFurther reflection

فَإِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا

QUR'AN · Ash-Sharh 94:5–6

Difficulty can narrow our view. A demanding season may begin to feel like the whole of life, leaving little space to notice the support, possibility or kindness around us.

In Ash-Sharh 94:5–6, the Qur’an reminds us that with hardship comes ease. Sitting with these verses gives us a reason to hold on to hope without denying the reality of what is difficult.

Hope can exist alongside practical action. We can ask for help, make thoughtful decisions, rest when we need to and keep turning to Allah. None of this requires us to pretend that a hard day is easy.

A pause for today

Identify one manageable next step. It may be a conversation you have been putting off, an appointment, a task broken into smaller parts or a simple act of care for yourself or someone else. Begin there, while making du’a for guidance and strength.

Carry the reminder with you

Read Surah Ash-Sharh in full and seek its explanation through reliable Tafseer. Let its message become a starting point for reflection, prayer and continued learning.
